THE ATTIC · file rescue

nothing leaves this machine

Every old format outlives the program that made it. A PCX from a 1994 scanner. A TGA from a DOS game. An IFF from Amiga paint software that no longer runs. Modern software looks at these and shrugs. Drop one here — it is identified from its bytes, not its extension, decoded in your browser, and can be rescued to PNG. No upload, no account, no server.

Why this exists. Digital preservation fails quietly: the files survive, the software does not. Every existing converter is paid, locked to one operating system, or makes you upload your archive to somebody else's server. This one is a static page with no network calls in it — you can read the source and confirm that before you drop anything sensitive in.

Decoders are specified in .kiro/specs/ and proven by tests/run.js. Formats: PCX (1985) · TGA (1984) · IFF ILBM (1985) with HAM and Extra-Half-Brite · IFF ANIM animations.
